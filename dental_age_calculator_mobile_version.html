How to use. Stage each of the seven mandibular teeth by tapping the stage letter that matches what you see on the panoramic radiograph. The calculator will automatically compute four dental age estimates: the classical Demirjian 1973 (7 teeth), the Thai Duangto 2016 (7 teeth), and the two Thai Duangto 2018 four-teeth methods. Methods that need teeth you have not yet staged will show a hint. Scores are sex-specific because girls reach the same dental developmental state at younger chronological ages than boys.

About this tool. Developed by Chanika Manmontri with the assistance of Claude (Anthropic), 2026. For education and research purposes only; not for clinical or forensic decision-making without verification against original source materials. Chronological age = (date of X-ray − date of birth) / 365.25. Last updated: 16 April 2026.

Valid age ranges. Demirjian 1973: 3.0–16.0 yr · Duangto 2016 (7 teeth): 6–15 yr · Duangto 2018 (4 teeth I and II): 7–15 yr. Results outside these ranges are flagged.

95% predictive intervals are computed as ±1.96 × SEP, where SEPs are taken from the original papers. Duangto 2016 reports 0.83 yr (M) / 0.90 yr (F) for the seven-teeth Thai equation and 0.87 yr (M) / 0.73 yr (F) for the classical Demirjian method when applied to the Thai validation sample. Duangto 2018 reports 0.818 / 0.766 yr for Method I and 0.843 / 0.790 yr for Method II.
